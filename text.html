Master (2021) is a Tamil action-thriller directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, starring Vijay as JD, an alcoholic professor assigned to a juvenile correction facility, where he clashes with Bhavani (Vijay Sethupathi), a ruthless gangster exploiting the young inmates for crime. The film unfolds as a gripping battle of ideologies between the two men, blending stylish action, sharp writing, and strong performances, backed by Anirudh Ravichander's music, and it went on to become a major commercial success and one of Vijay's most acclaimed films in recent years.
